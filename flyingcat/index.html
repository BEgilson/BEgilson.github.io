Privacy Policy – Flying Cat

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Flying Cat is a game for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac, made by Frontal Developments (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

In short: you can play without an account. If you make one, we store your game progress so it follows you to your other devices. On iPhone and iPad the game shows ads from Google AdMob. We do not sell your data.

Data on Your Device

Your progress (coins, best distance, number of flights, upgrades, skins, hats, accessories, scenery and daily reward streak) and your settings (sound and vibration) are saved on your device. The first time you play, the game asks the year you were born; that also stays on your device and is never sent to us. It is used only to set up ads and accounts for your age (see “Children and Teenagers” below). All of this stays until you delete the game.

Optional Account and Online Saves

Making an account is optional. If you sign in, we use Google Firebase to run your account and store your save:

  • Email and password: your email address and an account ID. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Sign in with Apple: an account ID and the email address Apple shares with us, which can be a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”.
  • Online save: the game progress listed above, linked to your account ID and stored in Cloud Firestore, so you can continue on another device.

When the game connects to Firebase, Google receives technical information such as your IP address to provide the service. See Privacy and security in Firebase. We use this data only to provide your account and saves. It is kept until you delete your account.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, your distances and achievements are sent to Apple. Your Game Center name and scores can be seen by other players on the leaderboards. If you allow access to your Game Center friends, the game uses it only to show the Friends leaderboard. Game Center is provided by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Advertising (iPhone and iPad Only)

The iPhone and iPad versions show ads from Google AdMob. The Apple TV and Mac versions have no ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when the game asks;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device (such as model and system version);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information.

All ads in the game are limited to content suitable for all ages. Players under 18 get Google’s child or teen ad treatment and are never asked to allow tracking (see “Children and Teenagers” below).

For players 13 and over in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for consent before ads are personalised, using Google’s consent form. Adults everywhere see Apple’s “Allow tracking?” prompt, which decides whether the advertising identifier can be used. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are less personalised.

You can change your ad consent at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices, and your tracking choice in your device’s Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Why We Use Your Data

  • To provide the game, your account and online saves: because you asked for them (performance of a contract).
  • Personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, security and preventing abuse: our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and working.

Sharing and International Transfers

We do not sell your data. It is processed by the service providers named above: Google (Firebase and AdMob) and Apple (Sign in with Apple and Game Center). They may process data outside your country, including in the United States, using legal safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Deleting Your Data

  • Account and online save: in the game, go to Account → Delete account. This deletes your account and your online save. If you used Sign in with Apple, the game’s access to your Apple ID is revoked as well.
  • Data on your device: delete the game.
  • You can also email us to ask for your data to be deleted.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, contact us at frontal.developments@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Iceland, Persónuvernd).

Children and Teenagers

Flying Cat is for players of all ages. Because of this, the game asks for the player’s birth year once, the first time it is played, and adjusts what it does:

  • Under 13: ads are child-safe and not personalised (Google’s child treatment, which follows the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act). The game does not ask to allow tracking, and online accounts are not available, so we do not collect any personal data from children such as an email address. Progress is saved only on the device.
  • 13 to 17: ads get Google’s teen treatment and the game does not ask to allow tracking. Players under 16 are marked as being under the age of digital consent, so their ads are not personalised. Online accounts are available.
  • 18 and over: as described in the rest of this policy.

Game Center accounts for children are managed by their parents through Apple’s Family Sharing. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also let you know in the game.
