Flying Cat Support

Flying Cat is a game for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, and Android phones, tablets and Android TV: slide down the slope, launch off the ramp and see how far your cat can fly. If something isn't working, or you have an idea for the game, we'd love to hear from you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

On phones and tablets, tap to start sliding, tap in the air to jump, and hold to use the jetpack. On Apple TV, click the remote's touch surface to slide and hold it for the jetpack, and press Play/Pause to jump. On Android TV, press OK to slide, tap OK in the air to jump and hold it for the jetpack. On Mac, click or press Space. Game controllers work on every device.

How do I fly further?

Every flight earns coins. Spend them in the shop on upgrades like the wingsuit, the jetpack, a bigger ramp and the sled. A flat landing counts as a perfect landing and gives bonus coins, and coming back every day gives a daily reward.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved on your device. An optional free account (Sign in with Apple or email) saves your progress online, so you can keep playing on your other devices, iPhone or Android. Online accounts are for players 13 and over. On Android TV, use an email account; Sign in with Apple isn't available there.

I signed out and my progress is gone

When you sign out, your progress is saved online and the game starts over on that device. Sign in again with the same account to get it back.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, open Account → Delete account. This deletes your account and your online save for good, and the game starts over. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game, and make sure you're signed in to the store with the account you bought it with: your Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, or your Google account on Android. The purchase works on all your devices from the same store, but one bought on the App Store doesn't carry over to Android, or the other way round.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or through Google Play's refund page for Android.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Leaderboards and achievements use Game Center on Apple devices and Google Play Games on Android. Make sure you're signed in (Game Center in your device's Settings app, or the Google Play Games app). On Android, your Play Games profile must be visible to be ranked on the worldwide leaderboard. Only your best distance is shown, and iPhone and Android players have separate leaderboards.

Can I change my ad privacy choices?

Yes. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU), open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. On iPhone and iPad, you can change whether apps may track you in the Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Settings app under Privacy → Ads.
