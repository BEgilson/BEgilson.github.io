Delete Your Flying Cat Account

Flying Cat is a game by Frontal Developments for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, Android and Android TV. An account is optional; this page explains how to delete one, and what happens to your data.

How to delete your account

In the game (fastest)

1. Open Flying Cat and tap Account.
2. Tap Delete account.
3. Confirm with your password, or with Apple if you signed in with Apple.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

Email frontal.developments@gmail.com from the email address of your account (or tell us the address you signed in with), with the subject "Delete my Flying Cat account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your account ID and email address.
  • Your online save: coins, best distance, number of flights, upgrades, skins, hats, accessories, scenery and daily reward streak.
  • If you used Sign in with Apple, the game's access to your Apple ID is revoked.
  • On the device you delete from, the game starts over.

Deleted data is removed from our systems straight away (within 30 days for requests by email). We don't keep backups or copies of it.

What is not deleted

  • Progress on other devices: it stays on those devices until you delete the game there.
  • Leaderboard scores and achievements belong to your Game Center or Google Play Games profile, which Apple and Google manage. You can remove them in those services' settings.
  • Purchases: Remove Ads belongs to your Apple Account or Google account, not to your Flying Cat account, so it still works after you delete your account.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under the Google Privacy Policy; you can reset your device's advertising ID in its settings.

More details are in our privacy policy. Questions? Visit support.
