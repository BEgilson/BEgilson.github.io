Privacy Policy – OCD Checklist

Last updated: September 23, 2026

OCD Checklist is designed with privacy in mind.

Information Collection

OCD Checklist does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Information entered into the app, including checklist items and completion records, is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Analytics and Advertising

OCD Checklist does not contain advertising and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

In-App Purchases

OCD Checklist may offer in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store users' payment information.

Medical Information

OCD Checklist is a personal organizational tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or privacy practices change.
